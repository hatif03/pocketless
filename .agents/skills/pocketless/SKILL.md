# Pocketless

A coworker that joins **their** Google Meet. Silent until addressed. Memory is the company.

## Surfaces

1. **Live session** (observer + Recall Output Media page)
2. **Person**
3. **Talk to Pocketless**
4. **internal Episode**

Hackathon demo: wake-name, a tool firing, a fact recalled from Priya’s March quote, person card updating after the Meet ends.

## Pipes

- **Now:** Recall.ai meeting bot, Google Meet only.
- **Later:** Zoom/Teams adapters, then companion. Same tools and DB.
